Your Matched Government Schemes

1. PMEGP — Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme

✓ Eligible

Your mobile shop in rural/semi-urban Odisha qualifies as a micro-enterprise under PMEGP. You can get a 25–35% margin money subsidy on your total project cost of ₹3 Lakh.

Why You're Eligible:
  • ✅ New business (greenfield enterprise)
  • ✅ Project cost within ₹10 Lakh (service sector limit)
  • ✅ Located in rural/semi-urban area
  • ✅ Indian citizen above 18 years
Subsidy25–35% of project cost
Your BenefitUp to ₹1.05 Lakh back
Bank PartnerSIDBI, PSU Banks
Apply Viakviconline.gov.in ↗

2. PM Mudra Loan — Kishore Category (₹50K–₹5 Lakh)

✓ Eligible

A collateral-free business loan ideal for your mobile shop. The Kishore category covers ₹50,000 to ₹5 Lakh. Use it to stock more phones or expand your accessory range.

Why You're Eligible:
  • ✅ Non-farm micro or small enterprise
  • ✅ Retail / Service business type matches
  • ✅ No prior loan default
  • ✅ No collateral required
Loan Limit₹50,000 – ₹5 Lakh
CollateralNone Required
Interest Rate~10–12% p.a.
Apply Viamudra.org.in ↗

3. Stand-Up India (For SC/ST or Women Entrepreneurs)

△ Check Eligibility

If you belong to SC/ST community or are a woman entrepreneur, Stand-Up India provides loans of ₹10 Lakh to ₹1 Crore at concessional rates for setting up new enterprises.

⚠️ This scheme applies only if you are SC/ST or a woman. Based on your profile as 'General' category, you may not be directly eligible. Verify with your nearest bank before applying.
Loan Range₹10 Lakh – ₹1 Crore
EligibilitySC/ST or Women only
Apply Viastandupmitra.in ↗
