Step-by-Step Business Launch Plan · Mobile Shop · Gopalpur, Cuttack

Your 6-Month Launch Roadmap

📅 WEEK 1–2
1

Apply for PMEGP at DIC Cuttack

Visit District Industries Centre, Cuttack (near Buxi Bazar). Ask for PMEGP application form for service sector. Carry: Aadhaar card, bank passbook, passport photo (3 copies), and a simple project report (you can use this analysis as your project summary).

📋 Documents: Aadhaar + Bank Passbook 📍 Where: DIC Cuttack 💰 Benefit: Up to ₹1.05L subsidy
2

Apply for Mudra Loan at SBI / Bank of India

Visit your nearest SBI or Bank of India branch. Ask for PM Mudra Yojana – Kishore category application. Request ₹1.5 Lakh. This is collateral-free and approved within 7–15 working days. Do this simultaneously with PMEGP.

📋 Documents: Aadhaar + PAN + Business plan 💰 Loan: Up to ₹1.5 Lakh, no collateral
📅 WEEK 3–4
3

Find a Shop & Register Your Business (GST)

Identify a 100–150 sq ft shop on the main road or near the market in Gopalpur. Negotiate 2-month advance + monthly rent. Simultaneously get a GST number online at gst.gov.in — mandatory for phone distributors. Also get Udyam Registration free at udyamregistration.gov.in.

🔑 GST Registration: Free online 🏪 Budget Rent: ₹8,000–12,000/month
📅 MONTH 1–2
4

Set Up Shop & Source Stock from Distributors

Set up display racks, CCTV camera, signboard. Contact these distributors in Cuttack for stock on 15-day credit: Balaji Mobile Distributors (Samsung, Realme) and Rajesh Electronics Wholesale (itel, Micromax). Start with ₹1.5L in phones + ₹40K in accessories. Buy repair tools from Cuttack electronics wholesale market.

📦 Initial Stock: ₹1.5L phones + ₹40K accessories 💡 Tip: Get 15-day credit from distributor
📅 MONTH 2–3
5

Launch & Build Local Reputation

Distribute 500 pamphlets in a 3km radius. Offer free screen protector with every phone purchase for the first month. Build a reputation for honest, quick repair — give receipts for every job with a 30-day work guarantee. Tell students in nearby schools and colleges about your shop through local WhatsApp groups.

📢 Marketing: Pamphlets + WhatsApp groups 🎁 Offer: Free screen protector with phone
📅 MONTH 3–6
6

Expand Services — EMI Partnership & DTH

Once your customer base is established, approach Bajaj Finance or TVS Credit for an EMI point partnership — this allows customers to buy phones on EMI from your shop, attracting ₹10K–₹20K phone buyers. Also register as a Jio / Airtel Retailer for recharge and SIM activation commission. These add ₹5,000–₹8,000/month extra revenue.

💳 EMI Point = Higher value customers 📲 Jio/Airtel Retailer = ₹5K+ extra/month
